1. The Problem Statement — what MoFPI
actually asked for
Inputs a solution must accept: commodity type, moisture content,
oil/fat content, pH, respiration rate, desired shelf life, storage
temperature, relative humidity, transport conditions, storage type
(ambient/chilled/frozen).
Outputs a solution must produce: recommended packaging
material (LDPE, HDPE, PET, metalized ﬁlms, aluminium foil laminates,
biodegradable ﬁlms, breathable ﬁlms) plus speciﬁcations — OTR,
WVTR, ﬁlm thickness, sealability, gas permeability, mechanical
strength, MAP suitability.
Optional extras the PS itself invites: shelf-life prediction,
sustainability analysis, cost optimization, QR-based traceability, eco-
friendly packaging recommendations.
Important: the oﬀicial brief does not mention regulatory compliance
anywhere. That’s our diﬀerentiator layer, not the literal ask — so the
core AI/ML recommendation engine must be real and demonstrable
on its own, with the compliance layer built on top.
2. Our Solution — the one-line version
A bilingual packaging recommendation engine that doesn’t stop
at “what material the science suggests” — it also tells the
processor exactly which FSSAI Schedule their food falls under,
which BIS IS code that material must legally meet, what their
label must legally say, and whether they need CPCB-EPR
registration. Built around real PMFME/ODOP commodities, in
Hindi and English, oﬄine-ﬁrst.
Three layers: 1. Core recommendation engine — satisﬁes the
literal PS ask (property-based material + spec recommendation) 2.
India Compliance Layer — our actual diﬀerentiator (FSSAI + BIS +
labelling + EPR + NABL, chained) 3. Accessibility layer —
Hindi/English, voice input, oﬄine-ﬁrst, PMFME/ODOP-speciﬁc seed
data
3. What Makes Us Unique —
distinguishing factors
The strongest known competitor (PackSmart AI — ML
recommendation, respiration/MAP science, shelf-life simulator, cost
calculator, sustainability scoring, PDF reports, farmer/researcher
modes) has zero mention of FSSAI, BIS, Legal Metrology, Hindi, or
any Indian regulation anywhere. That is the one clean, veriﬁed gap in
an otherwise sophisticated ﬁeld — don’t claim we’re more advanced
everywhere, claim we close the one gap nobody else has touched.

4.5 Recommendation engine — the honest version
Pick one, be able to explain it plainly under questioning: - Rule-based
scoring: weighted score of candidate materials against
moisture/fat/pH/respiration/shelf-life thresholds derived from seed
data. Not “AI” — say so if asked. Fast, transparent, explainable in one
sentence. - Lightweight ML: scikit-learn DecisionTreeClassifier or 
KNeighborsClassifier, trained on the seeded rows (expand to ~60–100
via small realistic jitter), predicting recommended_material. Report a
real accuracy number, however unglamorous.
Never claim deep learning or unexplained “AI-powered” — someone
will ask what the model actually is.
